THE STORY BEHIND GOLDEN DAYS

A FAIRBANKS
TRADITION.

Golden Days traces its roots to the discovery of gold that helped shape Fairbanks. Fifty years after Felix Pedro’s 1902 discovery, the community came together for the first Golden Days celebration in 1952. What started as a salute to Fairbanks’ Gold Rush history has grown into one of the city’s biggest summer traditions.

LOOK BACK THE HISTORY OF GOLDEN DAYS +

Golden Days celebrates the history of Fairbanks and the events that helped shape our community. In 1901, E.T. Barnette established a trading post along the Chena River. The following summer, Italian immigrant prospector Felix Pedro discovered paying quantities of gold about 12 miles north of the settlement.

News of the July 1902 discovery spread quickly, bringing prospectors to the Tanana Valley and helping transform the small trading post into the growing community of Fairbanks. Fifty years later, in 1952, Golden Days began as a community celebration honoring Fairbanks’ Gold Rush and pioneer heritage.

Over the decades, the celebration has grown to include the Grande Parade, downtown Street Fair, community events and the Rubber Duckie Race, which joined Golden Days in 1985.

The details change from year to year, but the heart of Golden Days remains the same: Fairbanks celebrating Fairbanks.
