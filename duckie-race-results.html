How to Claim Your Prize.

If your ticket number appears above, follow these steps to verify your ticket and coordinate prize pickup or shipping with Chamber staff.

01

Check Your Number

Confirm that your ticket number is listed above under the correct ticket color: pink or yellow.

02

Hold Onto Your Ticket

You will need your physical winning ticket and an ID to claim your prize. Winners outside Fairbanks may provide a clear photo or photocopy of the winning ticket and ID.

03

Pick Up Your Prize

Greater Fairbanks Chamber staff will contact winners to confirm their information and coordinate prize pickup or shipping.

04

Questions?

Email info@fairbankschamber.org or call (907) 452-1105.

CLAIM DEADLINE SEPTEMBER 31ST, 2027

Prizes may not be exchanged for cash. Winners receiving prizes valued over $600 will be required to complete applicable tax documentation.
