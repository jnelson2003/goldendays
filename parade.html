MANDATORY SAFETY MEETING

Tuesday, July 20, 2027, at 6:30 p.m. at the Morris Thompson Cultural and Visitors Center. Your entry will not be allowed to participate without a primary representative attending the meeting.

MINOR PARTICIPATION

All participants under the age of 18 must have a completed Parental Consent Form signed by a legal parent or guardian.

UNDER 18 ROSTER

All parade organizers must list every participant under the age of 18 on the parade roster.

BE READY BEFORE PARADE DAY

Complete required forms, review the parade guidelines and make sure your primary representative attends the mandatory safety meeting.

KNOW
THE ROUTE.

The Golden Days Grande Parade begins at the Carlson Center and proceeds downtown along 1st Avenue before turning right onto Barnette Street, left onto 2nd Avenue, and right onto Noble Street, ending at Co-op Market and Deli.
